# Рабочий стол Грата

Создан встроенным ImageGen. Два отдельных ассета: фон и атлас из шести иконок. Подписи, панель задач и кликабельные области рисует Godot, чтобы интерфейс оставался рабочим.

`wallpaper.png` — ночной город в тёмной сливово-синей палитре. `icons.png` — 1536 × 1024, сетка 3 × 2, ячейки 512 × 512. Слева направо: проект игры, Blender, ЖБТ; поиск, блокнот, Grok. Godot извлекает иконки через AtlasTexture без изменения исходника.

Запрос фона:

> Use case: ui-mockup. Asset type: background wallpaper for a fictional desktop operating system inside a pixel-art adventure game about a young amateur game developer at 03:47 at night. Generate ONLY the wallpaper artwork, no UI or icons or text. Wide landscape approx 16:9. Restrained cinematic pixel art, visibly crisp square pixel clusters, no photorealism. Deep charcoal-plum and midnight navy gradient sky with a softly glowing small pale moon in upper right, hazy muted violet city rooftops along the lower quarter, a few warm amber apartment windows. Subtle atmosphere of a quiet late-night computer session. Left two-thirds very dark and uncluttered with generous negative space for clickable desktop icons, right third atmospheric skyline and moon, lower edge darker to support taskbar. Sophisticated subdued colors matching a dark plum pixel art bedroom. Flat digital wallpaper, edge-to-edge, no monitor frame, no room, no people, no giant focal object, no lettering, no logos, no grid, no faux app windows, no icons, no watermark. This background will be used directly as a game texture.

Запрос иконок:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: one spritesheet of SIX matching fictional desktop application icons for a pixel-art adventure game, transparent PNG. Exact regular THREE COLUMNS by TWO ROWS grid on a landscape 3:2 canvas. Each equal square cell contains exactly one large centered icon with ample transparent padding (icon occupies central 65%); no overlap, identical apparent size. Ordered left to right: TOP ROW 1 a warm gold folder with a little old-fashioned door key emblem (My Game project folder); 2 an orange Blender-like swirling ring around a blue low-poly cube (3D modelling app); 3 a soft mint green chat bubble with a tiny white interwoven knot (JBT helpful AI). BOTTOM ROW 4 a pale blue magnifying glass over a small cream page (Search); 5 an ivory spiral notepad with dark violet lines and a muted gold pencil (Notepad); 6 a dark charcoal rounded square with a bold white diagonal orbital X-like symbol and restrained red-violet highlight (Grok AI). Crisp attractive hand-pixelled late-90s desktop icons, readable at 48x48 display, large deliberate square pixel clusters, restrained 4-6 colors per icon, dark clean silhouette and subtle highlights. Match cozy dark plum nighttime game palette. No text, no labels, no letters, no numbers, no background rectangles around the entire grid, no grid lines, no captions, no interface, no watermark, no glow outside icon silhouette. Real transparency outside all six icons. Keep exact equal-cell arrangement, do not draw a desktop background.
